The Concept

You shape the form.
We immortalize it in silver.

BISHU bridges the intimate world of personal making with time-honored foundry casting. You begin with an unformed block of blue jeweler's wax, shaving and sculpting every contour to match your own hands.

Once carved, your exact wax master is invested, burned out, and cast into solid sterling silver. The ridges, tool marks, and organic facets you create remain preserved forever in solid metal.

The Craft

The Three Stages

A seamless journey from malleable blue wax to finished heirloom jewelry.

Stage 01
Blue Jeweler's Wax Frames 001–054

01 · Carve

Blue jewelry wax. You shape, bevel, and carve your own ring using fine hand tools. The wax gives immediate tactile feedback, allowing organic forms or precise lines.

Stage 02
Molten Sterling Silver Frames 055–100

02 · Cast

The wax pattern is transformed through lost-wax casting into solid silver. Molten 925 sterling silver fills the inverted mold cavity, replacing the wax in exact detail.

Stage 03
Polished 925 Silver Frames 101–168

03 · Finish

The raw silver ring is refined, sanded, and polished into the finished piece. Rotary tools smooth the internal fit before a cotton buffing wheel imparts a luminous mirror sheen.

Raw Materials

Two Materials.
One Continuous Form.

The purity of the finished ring depends entirely on the integrity of the carving wax and the composition of the casting alloy.

Artisan Carving Wax

Specially formulated hard blue wax with minimal brittleness. Holds crisp razor cuts and smooth ergonomic contours without cracking or warping under hand warmth.

Solid 925 Sterling Silver

92.5% pure elemental silver blended with 7.5% copper for superior strength, tactile weight, and brilliant reflectivity when buffed.
